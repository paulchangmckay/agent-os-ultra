# Hexagram 38: K'uei / Opposition

## Core Idea
Two forces that live together but move in fundamentally different directions cannot unite for great undertakings, yet small, gradual reconciliation remains possible — and opposition itself is the necessary condition that eventually drives things back toward union.

## Trigrams
- Upper: Li (Fire, which burns upward)
- Lower: Tui (the Lake, the Joyous, which seeps downward)

## The Judgment
> OPPOSITION. In small matters, good fortune.

**Meaning**: When people are estranged and their points of view diverge too widely for a great common undertaking, forcing matters only deepens the rift — limiting oneself to gradual, small-scale effects is where success remains available.

## The Image
> Above, fire; below, the lake: the image of OPPOSITION. Thus amid all fellowship the superior man retains his individuality.

**Meaning**: Fire and water never mingle even in contact, each keeping its own nature — so a cultured person, even amid close association with very different others, never loses their own individuality to the crowd.

## The Lines
- **Nine at the beginning**: "Remorse disappears. If you lose your horse, do not run after it; it will come back of its own accord. When you see evil people, guard yourself against mistakes." — Do not force reconciliation; what belongs with you returns on its own, and what does not must simply be endured without provoking real hostility.
- **Nine in the second place**: "One meets his lord in a narrow street. No blame." — When formal meeting has become impossible due to misunderstanding, an informal, seemingly accidental encounter can still serve, provided real inner affinity exists.
- **Six in the third place**: "One sees the wagon dragged back, the oxen halted, a man's hair and nose cut off. Not a good beginning, but a good end." — Despite feeling thwarted, insulted, and hindered at every turn, cleaving to the one you truly belong with brings the situation to a good end.
- **Nine in the fourth place**: "Isolated through opposition, one meets a like-minded man with whom one can associate in good faith. Despite the danger, no blame." — Isolation among those from whom one is estranged is overcome by finding someone genuinely kindred, whose trust dissolves the danger.
- **Six in the fifth place**: "Remorse disappears. The companion bites his way through the wrappings. If one goes to him, how could it be a mistake?" — A sincere companion, though unrecognized at first through general estrangement, breaks through the obstruction; going to meet him when he does is simply one's duty.
- **Nine at the top**: "Isolated through opposition, one sees one's companion as a pig covered with dirt, as a wagon full of devils. First one draws a bow against him, then one lays the bow aside. He is not a robber; he will woo at the right time. As one goes, rain falls; then good fortune comes." — Misjudging a true friend as a threat, one arms oneself defensively, then recognizes the mistake and lays down the weapon; the tension breaks like rain relieving a storm's sultriness, since opposition at its climax turns to its opposite.

## Confucian Commentary Highlights
- The Commentary on the Decision offers a striking reframe: opposition is not merely an obstacle but the natural precondition for union — heaven and earth are opposites yet their action is concerted, man and woman are opposites yet strive for union, and it is precisely the differences between things that make it possible to classify and order the world.
- The hexagram is named for the divergent motion of its two trigrams — fire flaming upward, the lake's water seeping downward — and is explicitly noted as the structural inverse of Hexagram 37 (The Family), whose unity gives way to this hexagram's estrangement per the Sequence.
- Several line-pairs are shown as deliberate mirrors: the returning horse at the first line pairs with meeting a like-minded man at the fourth; "one meets his lord" at the second pairs with the companion "biting through the wrappings" at the fifth; "not a good beginning, but a good end" at the third pairs with the rain that resolves doubt at the top.
- The top line's imagery (pig, devils, bow) is explained structurally through the nuclear trigram K'an (associated with pigs, wagons, cunning, danger, robbers) — clarifying that what looks like a hostile omen is, once correspondence with the third line is understood, actually a well-meant approach misperceived as a threat.

## Worked Example
Consider the nine at the top: "Isolated through opposition, one sees one's companion as a pig covered with dirt... First one draws a bow against him, then one lays the bow aside... As one goes, rain falls; then good fortune comes." Someone deep in a long estrangement — a falling-out with a colleague or old friend — may come to see the other party as fundamentally hostile, arming themselves emotionally against an imagined attack. This line shows the resolution: recognizing that the perceived threat was never real, laying down the defense, and allowing the tension to break like a cleared storm. It extends the Judgment's claim that only "small matters" succeed under opposition by showing the exact turning point at which even a long, personal opposition can dissolve — not through victory over the other, but through recognizing the misunderstanding for what it was.

## Key Takeaways
- Do not chase reconciliation by force — let what genuinely belongs with you return on its own timetable.
- When you cannot avoid people who are misaligned with you, endure them without provoking open hostility; forcing them away creates the very conflict you want to avoid.
- Hold on to a right relationship even when circumstances make you look thwarted or humiliated — the situation can still end well.
- Seek out and trust a genuinely kindred person even amid a larger group of estrangement; that one relationship can neutralize your isolation.
- When you find yourself defensively bracing against someone you have misjudged as hostile, check the assumption before acting on it — opposition carried to its extreme often flips into its opposite.
